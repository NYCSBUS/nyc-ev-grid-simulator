Overview

The Senior Specialist for EV Program Strategy & Performance Analytics in the EV Strategy group supports the planning, development, execution, tracking and monitoring of the Electric Vehicle ("EV") near-term strategy to ensure Con Edison and New York State clean transportation and e-mobility goals and targets are achieved. The Senior Specialist is responsible for conducting analyses, developing and owning analytics tools, program tracking and optimization, incorporating market intelligence, and partnering with staff across the Department and external stakeholders to ensure successful program delivery.

Responsibilities

Core Responsibilities

The Senior Specialist for EV Program Strategy & Performance Analytics in the EV Strategy group supports the planning, development, execution, tracking and monitoring of the Electric Vehicle ("EV") near-term strategy to ensure Con Edison and New York State clean transportation and e-mobility goals and targets are achieved.
The Senior Specialist is responsible for conducting analyses, developing and owning analytics tools, program tracking and optimization, incorporating market intelligence, and partnering with staff across the Department and external stakeholders to ensure successful program delivery.
Work with the EV teams and other internal stakeholders to develop, prioritize, and implement strategic initiatives.
Support internal program teams to define metrics to track for program and market intelligence and for performance optimization.
Collaborate with EV teams to identify, structure and prioritize gaps, challenges, opportunities, and proposed solutions.
Collaborate with internal teams to develop effective strategic and tactical adjustments in response to changes to program, budgetary and regulatory needs, and market changes.
Create and own tools and analyses for program optimization, e.g. cost validation, ideal site identification, budget monitoring, incentive setting, target development, and/or predictive tools for pipeline management. Opportunity to build in automation via machine learning and/or advanced algorithms.
Develop knowledge of and skills working with EV data and databases, both internal and external.
Monitor EV market demand and trends along with program performance to inform program strategy.
May include representing the Companys interest and communication of EV Program status to external stakeholders such as the New York State Department of Public Service Staff and other industry stakeholders.
Support forecasting of EV program performance and EV load.
Work effectively and lead cross-functional teams responsible for new department initiatives that may arise from time to time.
Perform other related tasks and assignments as required.

Ability to deal with ambiguity and work comfortably in a dynamic environment, required.
High proficiency in the Microsoft Office suite, including Outlook, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Project, required.
Ability to make decisions in real time under pressure, required.
Intellectual curiosity and enthusiasm for learning new skills, required.

Strong presentation and writing skills, preferred.
Demonstrated ability to prioritize, analyze, and solve complex strategic and tactical opportunities, preferred.

Demonstrated organizational skills and the ability to see the big picture while maintaining attention to detail, preferred.
